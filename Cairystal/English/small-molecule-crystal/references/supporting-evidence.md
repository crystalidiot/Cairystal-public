# Keep supporting experiments separate from the Bragg model

Read only when a crystallographic report or learning source makes property, microscopy, phase-transition, spectroscopy or computational claims. These observations can constrain structural hypotheses; they do not substitute for atom/reflection validation.

## Specimen, phase and time

Match batch, selected crystal, temperature, thermal direction/cycle, mother liquor, drying, pressure, grinding, annealing/poling and illumination. Powder, pellet, film, dissolved/recast material and the original crystal are not interchangeable specimens. Recrystallizing after dissolution is not demonstrated same-crystal reversibility; a visually intact particle need not remain a single crystal.

Distinguish single-crystal atomic refinement, powder Rietveld models, Pawley/Le Bail fits, tentative indexing, analogue proposals and calculated structures. A failed high-temperature measurement remains unsolved unless later evidence resolves it. Similar cells/space groups do not uniquely identify polymorphs; identical groups can occur across a real transition. Different compositions are not polymorphs of one compound.

For photoreactivity, distinguish in-situ same-specimen data from recrystallized products or powder assignments. Molecular proximity, bending or fluorescence alone does not justify product bonds or conversion SOFs. Macroscopic bending reversal does not prove chemical reversal.

## Disorder, domains and symmetry

Average split sites, partial occupancy, dynamic hopping, static disorder, separate phases, stacking faults and twin domains are different models. Do not derive PART/SOF/BASF from entropy state counts, spectroscopy populations, optical colors or simulated trajectories. Symmetry-related possibilities need not be experimentally resolved or equally populated.

Polar, noncentrosymmetric, Sohncke, molecularly chiral and switchably ferroelectric are different classifications. Optical-rotation sign, uncalibrated CD, habit, SHG/THG absence, PFM contrast or a hysteresis loop cannot independently choose a space group. Local/topological ideal symmetry is not crystallographic site symmetry. Preserve diffuse/satellite evidence and explicit alternative models.

For microscopy/video, record parent recording, specimen, interval, crop, scale and playback speed. Different hashes/exports are not new experiments. Container time, embedded clock and temperature/load record differ; accelerated playback is not kinetic time. Check sequential decode coverage when duration is inconsistent. Retain cracks, specimen motion and illumination as competing interpretations; static samples do not establish what happened between them or in the audio.

## Chemistry and spectroscopy

Feed ratios, bulk elemental analysis, solution ee/d.r., mass-spectral adducts, spectral component areas and selected-crystal occupancies have distinct meanings and response assumptions. Separate neutral formula mass, monoisotopic m/z and adduct identity. TGA loss/residue assignments require mass balance, not only a plausible percentage.

Diffraction may not uniquely resolve protonation, radical/non-innocent ligand states or valence tautomerism. Reconcile counterions, actual connectivity and independent specimen-matched evidence. Electronic resonance/spin populations are not nuclear-site disorder. For spin crossover compare corresponding individual sites, geometry and uncertainties rather than imposing common bond targets.

After coordinated-solvent loss, reassess the coordination sphere: an unchanged-count counterion may become bound. Distinguish coordinated from lattice solvent, and unlocated chemically supported H from invented coordinates.

## Calculations, cells and units

Keep DFT/geometry-optimized/ordered-P1/drawing models separate from experimental coordinates, ADPs and structure-factor submissions. Artificial vacuum, hidden atoms, altered display U and simplified occupancies belong only to the labeled calculation/display copy. Similarity scores require atom/component scope, basis, tolerance and sampling; they are not validation.

Transform cells, positions, cages and ADP tensors consistently before comparing displacements or strain. Fractional-coordinate differences across different cells are not Angstrom shifts. Preserve reference phase, orthonormal frame, tensor/shear convention and uncertainty; small-angle formulas use radians. Direct [uvw] lengths differ from reciprocal d(hkl).

For orientation-dependent spontaneous strain, distinguish the symmetry-state average in one frame from a measured domain-population average. Group/subgroup and translation indices, not raw conventional Z ratios, govern domain possibilities. Compliance is not modulus.

Compare energies only with compatible composition, cell size, reference state and per-cell/molecule/area normalization. Electrostatic potential extrema, orbital gaps, optical gaps and activation energies are not interchangeable because all use eV. ELF lobes and effective point-charge framework centers are not extra atoms, metal oxidation states or occupancies. Validate formulas, units and cell metric before reusing source scripts; no blind execution of deletion/eval/overwrite helpers.

## Property figures and interpretation

Preserve native chart caches, axes, units, clipping and duplicate traces, while distinguishing caches from verified raw acquisition. Image crops are not independent specimens; recalculate scale bars after resizing. Blank tracker cells mean unknown, not a negative experiment.

Contact-area percentages are not occupancies or bond energies. Define void model/probe/grid and volume normalization; do not normalize implausible totals merely to reach 100%. A database's common geometry or docking score is not a restraint target.

Distinguish current from current density, maximum/switching/breakdown fields, specimen geometry, frequency and thermal history. A smaller total current does not prove lower leakage. Preserve conduction/background alternatives when interpreting polarization. Along a given path, integrals of E dP and P dE are related by the endpoint change of EP, not generally interchangeable. Keep direct calorimetry separate from indirect equilibrium estimates and normalize heat capacity/entropy consistently.

Distinguish transition entropy from fusion entropy and enthalpy; an effective exp(ΔS/R) state ratio requires normalization/independence assumptions and is not an instruction to create that many PARTs. Decomposition or specimen degradation can defeat a reversible-phase interpretation.

Source contradictions, withdrawn explanations and updated conclusions stay traceable in private case notes. A reviewer suggestion is not an experiment, an author reply is not independent validation, and neither authorizes data removal or model changes.
